An overview of how this early AI smile visualisation service should be used.

DEVELOPMENT DRAFT · Updated 26 October 2026 · Not a final legal notice for a live patient-data service.

1. What the service does

Smile Labs Digital provides demonstration AI visualisations for teeth whitening and missing teeth restoration. These illustrations are not diagnoses, treatment plans or promises of achievable clinical outcomes.

2. Image accuracy and professional advice

AI may alter existing teeth, gums, facial features or backgrounds and may add inaccurate anatomy. Always have any planned treatment assessed by a qualified dental professional.

3. Image rights and permitted uploads

Only upload photographs you are entitled to use. This is a development prototype: please do not upload identifiable patient images or sensitive health information. Dental professionals must separately meet patient information, confidentiality and legal requirements before any future real-patient use is enabled.

4. Accounts and preview allowances

The prototype uses email-code verification. Individual users currently have one preview; approved dental professional accounts currently have ten per calendar month. A preview may be unavailable due to network, model, credit or service errors.

This is an evolving development preview without any promise of continuous availability or error-free output.
