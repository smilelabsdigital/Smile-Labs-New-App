Privacy Policy

Smile Labs Digital · Development draft · 26 October 2026

What we collect

We collect registration details such as your name, email address, telephone number and, where requested, account or practice details. When you choose to make a cosmetic preview, we process your uploaded photograph.

How your photo is used

With your explicit permission, your photograph is sent securely to OpenAI through its image-editing API to create the cosmetic preview you request. We do not use your photograph for advertising without separate permission. AI previews are not dental advice.

Photo storage

Our application processes original and generated photographs in memory during the request and returns the preview to your browser; it does not intentionally save image files or keep a photo gallery on our application server. The original and generated images remain visible in your browser until cleared, refreshed or closed. Select Clear to remove them from the page. HTTPS must be configured on the deployed site.

Important: OpenAI may retain data under its own API policies. Its standard abuse-monitoring retention is generally up to 30 days, with exceptions. The actual processing region and any international transfers depend on the provider and account configuration and must be confirmed before launch; UK-only processing is not currently promised.

Consent and your choices

The photo consent box is unticked by default and must be selected before choosing or taking a photo. Untick it or clear the photograph to stop further use in the current browser session. You can contact us about withdrawal of consent and requests for access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Withdrawal cannot reverse processing already completed.

Account data

Registration and account usage information are currently kept in a server-side JSON file for this development version. Production security, retention periods, hosting location, lawful bases and any additional health-data requirements need final review before real patient use.

Dental photographs

This development version is not approved for identifiable patient images. Dental practices need appropriate patient permissions, a documented lawful basis, safeguards and, where required, a data processing agreement before uploading patient images.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: info@smilelabsdigital.co.uk. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Before public launch

Confirm the legal company name and business address, precise OpenAI processing region and retention settings, international transfer safeguards, lawful bases, additional conditions for dental health data, and retention/deletion practices. This is a short development draft, not a final legal notice.
