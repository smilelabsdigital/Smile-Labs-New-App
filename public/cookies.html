Cookie Information

What the current app uses to keep you signed in, and what we still need to confirm.

DEVELOPMENT DRAFT · Updated 26 October 2026 · Not a final legal notice for a live patient-data service.

1. Essential session cookie

The application currently sets sl_session after verification. It is an HTTP-only cookie that supports account sign-in and expires after up to seven days. The production deployment must enforce the Secure flag over HTTPS.

2. Analytics and other cookies

This version of the application does not intentionally add separate advertising or analytics cookies in its own code. Third-party hosting, embedded functionality or future integrations may change this; a production cookie audit is required before publication.

3. Cookie choices

Essential security and session cookies are needed for the login feature. If we introduce non-essential analytics or advertising cookies, we will provide appropriate consent controls before setting them. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

4. Questions

Contact info@smilelabsdigital.co.uk for details.

Confirm the operating legal entity, registered address, lawful bases and special-category conditions, data retention and deletion arrangements, subprocessors, international transfers and data protection contracts. This prototype must not be used with real patient health photographs.
